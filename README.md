# SpeakUp

An open-source AI speaking coach that helps you become a fluent
English speaker by practicing real conversations.

## How it works
1. **Listen**: the app hears what your conversation partner says.
2. **Reply**: AI writes a polished, high-level response (IELTS band 9
   style) in text only. No audio is generated for you.
3. **Speak**: you read the reply aloud.
4. **Score**: AI evaluates your pronunciation word by word.

## Why
Reading advanced replies out loud trains your tongue and, over time,
builds vocabulary and grammar naturally, without rote memorization.

## Features (planned)
- Speech-to-text with Whisper
- Replies adjustable from B1 to C2
- Per-word pronunciation feedback
- Spaced repetition for new vocabulary
